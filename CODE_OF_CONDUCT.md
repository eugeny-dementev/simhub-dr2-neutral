# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn when mistakes happen.
- Focus on what is best for the community and its users.

Harassment, sexualized attention, trolling, insults, threats, deliberate
intimidation, publishing private information, and other conduct inappropriate
in a professional setting are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report abusive behavior privately to `eugeny.dementev@ya.ru`. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and fairly, with confidentiality respected as far as
practical. Maintainers may remove content or temporarily or permanently ban
participants for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
